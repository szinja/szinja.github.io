Download résumé

About

I'm a researcher applying machine learning to cancer evolution and treatment resistance prediction — work that only matters if it can hold up outside a notebook. My focus is trustworthy, responsible AI in clinical and public health contexts: closing the gap between a model that performs well and one that can actually be deployed and relied on.

My MSc dissertation built a probabilistic multimodal survival model for cancer patients, combining Tree-LSTM embeddings of tumor phylogenies with clinical covariates on the TRACERx NSCLC cohort. Before this, I spent three years at Twitter working on recommendation systems and distributed ML infrastructure.
